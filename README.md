### 🌾 [FarmWise](https://github.com/gangadhar-sivaneni/farmwise)
**Your field, mapped from space: AI crop scans, live mandi prices, weather & profit planning, in English and తెలుగు.**

- 🗺️ **Satellite field mapping:** draw a plot; real acreage is computed from coordinates (Turf.js)
- 📸 **AI crop-health scan** with Google Gemini vision, answers in Telugu too
- 📈 **Live mandi prices** from Agmarknet + plot-specific **weather & profit** planning
- 🔐 **Firebase Auth + Realtime DB:** Google sign-in, email verification, per-user data
- ☁️ Deployed on **Vercel** with serverless APIs
